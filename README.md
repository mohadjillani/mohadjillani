Seven-plus years building web products end to end: I take a business problem, turn it into an architecture, build it with a team, and stay accountable for how it behaves in production.

## How I work

1. **Understand the business problem first.** The right architecture depends on what the company is actually trying to win.
2. **Design for the team that maintains it.** Boring, readable, documented — over clever.
3. **Ship incrementally.** Working software in front of stakeholders early, then iterate.
4. **Treat performance and security as requirements**, not a phase at the end.
5. **Automate the repetitive parts** — CI, deployments, checks that a human would eventually forget.
6. **Watch production.** Logging and monitoring are part of the feature, not an afterthought.
7. **Write down the decisions that matter**, so the next engineer knows why, not just what.
8. **Balance quality against the date.** Both are real constraints; pretending otherwise helps nobody.
